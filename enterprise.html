SHEET G-002

Four businesses, one platform under your own brand

The same four verticals OMEGA is built around, deployed in your environment and wearing your name. What changes per vertical is which part of the platform your people live in all day.

OEM & technology channel

Put your dealers and integrators on a platform that specifies your equipment by default — your catalogue, your branding, your channel quoting from one set of numbers instead of six spreadsheets.

Developers, owners & capital

One standard way to screen, size and underwrite every site in the book. The model does the work an analyst currently redoes per deal, and it does it the same way every time.

EPC & engineering services

Internalise design, drawings, permitting and take-off across the project book — the engineering you currently buy out, produced in-house and issued on your own title block.

Installers & sales channel

Every rep gets the same sizing engine and the same proposal, branded as yours, running on whatever they carry into the field. No install, nothing to sync when they get back.

Not a vertical

Municipalities and AHJs

They are a counterparty, not a customer vertical — they review your permits alongside you, in the AHJ Approval Portal.
