Where storage permits get reviewed and approved

The AHJ Approval Portal is a shared workspace for the permitting coalition — municipalities, zoning boards, and authorities having jurisdiction review, comment on, and approve energy storage permits in one place, working from the same permit-ready packages OMEGA produces.

Developers submit permit-ready packages generated in OMEGA; authorities review, comment, and approve in the same workspace — no email chains, no version confusion.
