SHEET G-005

Pre-screening to funded, on one record

Each step reads what the step before it produced. Nothing is re-keyed, re-drawn or re-modelled — which is the only reason the numbers still agree by the time a lender sees them.

01

Pre-screen

Rank a parcel on grid position before anyone options the dirt — voltage, distance to the point of interconnection, circuits and buildable acreage.

02

Design and size

Lay the site out against what the utility can actually deliver, with the bill of materials writing itself as equipment lands.

03

Price

Quantities measured off the drawing, and a pro forma that reconciles to an hour-by-hour dispatch rather than to a peak.

04

Sell or source

Route the bill of materials to vendors for quotes, place compute capacity, or put the project itself in front of buyers.

05

Finance

File once with the evidence attached — or apply for financing straight from the project, against the numbers already on the record.

06

Operate

Enroll the asset across the value stack and keep earning on it once it is built.
