SHEET P-402

Tools that were used before they were sold

Most development software is designed by people who have never had to get a project financed. Ours is the other way round — every screen exists because a deal needed it, and the ten years of performance history behind the model is our own operating data, not a licensed dataset.

  • Storage stacking several revenue layers on one asset rather than one.
  • EV charging buffered by storage, where the grid connection would otherwise set the ceiling.
  • Microgrids and behind-the-meter systems sized against real interval data.
  • Compute campuses, where firm power and interconnection are worth more than the land.
What you get from it

The same numbers we underwrite with

When OMEGA screens a parcel, models a dispatch or reconciles a pro forma, it is running the method we use on our own capital. That is the whole claim — and it is why the platform is opinionated about interconnection headroom, hour-by-hour dispatch and evidence a lender will actually accept.
