SHEET P-402

480 kW charging, SDVPP and PJM frequency regulation on one asset

ClearSky’s Fulton, Illinois site pairs a battery energy storage system with 480 kW of DC fast charging — delivering high-power charging without triggering a costly utility service upgrade. The same battery is enrolled in ComEd’s Smart Distributed Virtual Power Plant and bids into PJM frequency regulation.

  • 480 kW DC fast charging — BESS-buffered, no grid upgrade required.
  • ComEd SDVPP enrollment — aggregated capacity and demand response.
  • PJM frequency regulation — sub-second injection into the RegD signal.
