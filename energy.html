Flagship project · Fulton, Illinois

480 kW charging, SDVPP & PJM frequency regulation on one asset

ClearSky's Fulton, Illinois site pairs a battery energy storage system with 480 kW of DC fast charging — delivering high-power charging without triggering a costly utility service upgrade. The same battery is enrolled in ComEd's Smart Distributed Virtual Power Plant program and injects into PJM's frequency regulation market, stacking three revenue layers on a single asset.

480 kW DC fast charging — BESS-buffered, no grid upgrade required.
ComEd SDVPP enrollment — aggregated capacity & demand response.
PJM frequency regulation — sub-second injection into the RegD signal.
