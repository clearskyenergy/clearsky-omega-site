SHEET M-302

Every deal, priced on the same evidence

Sponsors post projects with the utility data attached. Capital partners screen the same numbers, in the same format, across every state — capital ask, $/W, IRR, DSCR, stage and outcome on one row. No spreadsheets going back and forth.

If you build or originate

One form captures what a capital partner screens on first: state, technology, nameplate and storage, capital ask, proposed offtake rate and structure. Development stage, site control, interconnection and permits are captured up front, because partners screen on them.

If you deploy capital

Filter the book live by state, technology, outcome, sponsor, organization or deal size. Portfolio math sits at the top — deals in market, total pipeline, median check, blended $/W, bills on file and average sponsor IRR.
