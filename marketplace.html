SHEET M-302

Five steps, one file

Each step reads what the step before it produced. Nothing is re-keyed, re-drawn or re-modelled, which is the only reason the numbers still agree by the time a lender sees them.

01

Pre-screen the site

Rank a parcel on grid position before anyone options the dirt — voltage, distance to the point of interconnection, circuits, buildable acreage and setbacks.

02

Design and size it

Lay the site out against what the utility can actually deliver, with the bill of materials writing itself as you place equipment.

03

Price it honestly

Quantities measured off the drawing, not estimated — and a pro forma that reconciles to an hour-by-hour dispatch rather than to a peak.

04

Sell it or source it

Route the BOM to vendors for quotes, place compute capacity, or put the project itself in front of buyers.

05

Finance it

File once with the evidence attached and put it in front of capital partners — or apply for financing straight from the project.

06

Operate it

Enroll the asset across the value stack and keep earning on it once it is built.

SHEET M-303

Every deal, priced on the same evidence

Sponsors post projects with the utility data attached. Capital partners screen the same numbers, in the same format, across every state — capital ask, $/W, IRR, DSCR, stage and outcome on one row.

If you build or originate

One form captures what a capital partner screens on first: state, technology, nameplate and storage, capital ask, proposed offtake rate and structure — with development stage, site control, interconnection and permits captured up front, because partners screen on them.

If you deploy capital

Filter the book live by state, technology, outcome, sponsor or deal size. Portfolio math sits at the top — deals in market, total pipeline, median check, blended $/W, bills on file and average sponsor IRR.

Procurement and dispatch

Equipment and revenue, on the same record

Pool demand, sharpen pricing

Connect projects to vetted equipment and materials, then aggregate volume across the portfolio to negotiate pricing and terms no single project could reach alone.

One fleet, every revenue layer

Enroll and orchestrate assets across capacity, grid injection, frequency regulation and demand response — dispatched in real time so each asset earns the most the market will pay.
