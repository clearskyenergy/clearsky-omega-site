If you build or originate

File the deal once — the bills travel with it

One form captures what a capital partner screens on first: state, technology, nameplate and storage, capital ask, proposed offtake rate and structure. Development stage, site control, interconnection and permits are captured up front, because partners screen on maturity as hard as they screen on size.

Utility bills are the differentiator — twelve months of statements get a deal looked at roughly twice as fast, and size the storage straight from the interval data.
Not sized yet? File it anyway — an early opportunity can go in without a nameplate and be completed as it firms up.

If you deploy capital

Screen the whole market on one row format

Filter the book live by state, technology, outcome, sponsor, organization or deal size. Portfolio math sits at the top — deals in market, total pipeline, median check, blended $/W, bills on file and average sponsor IRR — and every deal opens onto the panel a credit screen actually asks for.

Offers are priced, not described — amount, instrument, rate, term, expiry and conditions precedent as checkboxes.
Term sheets stay in your storage — link a memo from Drive or Dropbox; nothing is copied onto the platform.
The rate equity screen — each proposed offtake rate against the blended rate from the host's own bills, so you can see who keeps the savings.
